group

Automated Software Testing (AST) group at fortiss and TUM.

Our research sits at the intersection of software engineering and AI. We build testing techniques that make AI-enabled systems, from deep learning components to autonomous vehicles and LLM-based applications, robust, reliable, and safe. Our tools and replication packages are on GitHub.
